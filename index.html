iTang Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

iTang respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, track, sell, or share personal data.

PDF documents selected by the user are processed locally on the device. Their contents are not uploaded to the developer or stored on external servers.

iTang uses Apple’s built-in text-to-speech technology. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, user accounts, or third-party data-collection services.

The user controls which documents are opened and may remove them from the device at any time.
